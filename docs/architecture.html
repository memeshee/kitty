Kitty · Nimiq Pay Mini App

Where the money can go, and who can stop it

One rule decides everything: coins move from the giver's wallet to the owner's wallet on Nimiq. Kitty's own servers only ever read. There is no line from our infrastructure to anyone's money, because there is no such line to draw.

Two minutes to verify: the goal page prints the owner's live balance next to the sum of its receipts.
